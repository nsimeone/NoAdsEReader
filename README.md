## 🔒 Privacy
This app has no internet access and collects no data. Books you add are copied into the app's private storage on your phone, and nothing leaves it.

---

## 📱 How to Install (APK)

Since this is a direct release, you can install the app easily on your Android device:

1. Download `app-release.apk` from the [Latest Release Page](https://github.com/nsimeone/NoAdsEReader/releases/latest).
2. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
3. If prompted, allow your browser or file manager to **install apps from unknown sources** when your phone asks for permission.
4. Tap **Install** and enjoy reading!
